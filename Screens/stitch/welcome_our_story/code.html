A Love Story

It began with a chance encounter in a dimly lit jazz club in New York City. The music was loud, the room crowded, yet when our eyes met, everything else faded away into a cinematic blur. That evening, over shared laughter and improvised melodies, we knew this was the start of something profound.

Five years, three continents, and countless cups of coffee later, we are ready to turn the page to our next chapter. We've built a life anchored in mutual respect, shared passions for design and travel, and an unwavering commitment to each other's growth.

Now, as we prepare to stand before our closest friends and family, we reflect on the beautiful, serendipitous journey that brought us here. We invite you to be part of the most important day of our lives.
